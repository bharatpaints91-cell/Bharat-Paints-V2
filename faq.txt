Q: What is Duco finish?
A: Duco is a premium spray paint finish for wood and MDF. Smooth, hard, washable surface. Available in any colour from RAL and NCS shade cards. Popular for kitchen cabinets, modular furniture and doors.

Q: What is PU coating for wood?
A: PU (Polyurethane) is a hard, durable topcoat for wood. Available in Matt, Satin and Gloss finish. Waterproof, scratch resistant, long lasting. Must apply Epoxy Clear Sealer first.

Q: What is Epoxy Clear Sealer?
A: Epoxy Clear Sealer is applied on wood before PU coating. It seals the wood grain and gives PU a strong base to bond with. Skipping this step causes PU to peel — very important first step.
